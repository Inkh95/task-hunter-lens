# Task Hunter Lens

One line: a small decision aid that turns a paid-task listing into a transparent, actionable priority.

## The Unique Kernel
Rank opportunities by a conservative expected hourly return: estimated chance of payment × net reward ÷ total time. Every input and assumption is visible and editable; uncertainty is called out rather than disguised as a prediction.

## Who It's For
An independent online worker comparing short paid tasks whose payout and effort are uncertain.

## The Core Loop
Paste a task title and link, enter reward, estimated hours, probability of acceptance and payout, and any fee. See a ranked score and why, then keep or discard the task.

## Inspiration & Identity
Fast, serious, clear. No forced recommendations. The concept is based on a paid-task prioritization problem rather than an existing app's code.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
A reliable comparison can help someone focus effort on realistic opportunities rather than headline rewards.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Two example tasks with different headline rewards can be compared in one screen. A lower reward may rank higher once realistic time, fees, and payment uncertainty are included. The user can change a value and see the order and explanation update.

## The POC Boundary
Manual entry, transparent scoring, local persistence, and status tracking. No account or external API needed.

## Later
Verified platform feeds, evidence attached to payout assumptions, and reminders.

## Explicitly Cut
Automatic scraping and claiming: platform permissions and verification would dominate this short build. Automatic applications: they require user-specific truthful submissions. Guaranteed earning claims: the estimates are subjective.
